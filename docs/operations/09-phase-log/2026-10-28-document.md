# Document space

**Run:** 2026-10-28 (one goal, after the brand-renderer merge) · **Plan:** [[_files/plan-2026-10-28-document|plan-2026-10-28-document]] (the live copy is `.kol/llm-context/plan-2026-10-28-document.md`; W4 and W5 are still open there) · **State:** W1–W3 built and reviewed (*"ok love it, I think this is fine for now"*), W5 shipped and the ticket closed 2026-10-28; Round 23 ruled the same day — Public Sans, like the reference.

## What it is

kol-website's `MarkdownPage` ticket: brand needs a folder of `.md` files rendered as pages in `SiteLayout` with a contents page, and no reader in the estate fits. The user's three rulings shaped it: an apps/* space first (*"this ticket needs talking about, we should make an apps/* space for this"*), the examples from kol-atlas (*"the only thing/content wise you need to refference is already in kol-atlas"*), and for kol-website only (*"this is not for kol-atlas, its for website"*). kol-theme already carried kol-atlas's document styles as `.kol-sheet`; the gap was that nothing in the DS turned markdown into that markup.
