**The plan (user go, 2026-09-26):**
1. `AppHub` — one app description in: name · subtitle · logomark · links · about · rail items · home (Catalog props) · settings sections · shortcuts · walkthrough steps · themeToggle node.

3. Keys wired by the Hub: `,` settings toggle · `S` shortcuts sheet · `\` rail · ⌥-digits across every rail row incl. Settings.
4. Walkthrough opt-in (Home's button) · its X inside the card (`WalkthroughPanel onClose`).
5. Settings tabs standard: Settings · About · Repo; Shortcuts block from the one array; ThemeToggle in the masthead cluster.
6. Parts exported too (Home · Settings · keys) for an app that takes one.
7. `apps/shell` — the reference app on `AppHub`, a placeholder tool; `pnpm shell`.
8. Refine `16-app-anatomy.md` table to match.
