2026-08-31, after closing two lobby tickets: *"i cant read your output its a
mess to decipher."* The replies were multi-paragraph, bolded throughout, with
tables and sub-sections — the long-form report shape, fired on work that needed
two lines.

**Why:** the global rule is already "default reply under 10 lines, long form is
opt-in only." Closing a ticket is not a report he asked for. The detail belongs
in the ticket's resolution and the CHANGELOG — both of which he can open — not
repeated in the terminal.

**How to apply:** after shipping a ticket, say what shipped and at what version,
plus anything genuinely held for him, in a few plain sentences. No tables, no
bold-per-line, no `###` headers, no verification matrices, no "known gaps"
sections. If he wants the detail he says so. See [[ask-before-acting-both-ways]]
and [[tmpl-prefix-standardised-outputs]].
