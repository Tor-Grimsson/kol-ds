## Behavior

**THE edge drawer**: a portalled panel sliding in from the left or right
viewport edge over a dimming backdrop — distinct from Modal (centered
prompt/confirm) and FullscreenOverlay (fills the viewport). Escape, backdrop
click and the built-in close button all call `onClose`; body scroll locks
while open; focus moves in, is trapped, and returns to the opener on close.
The slide transition is gated on `prefers-reduced-motion` — reduced users get
an instant mount with no transform animation.

## Rail and overlay

Anything docked to an edge — left, right, top or bottom — that collapses and expands is a **rail**; anything
floating over the page that closes is an **overlay**. `mode="over"` (the default) slides the rail over the page on a
scrim, with focus trap, Escape and scroll lock. `mode="push"` sits it in the layout: `open` expands it, closed it
collapses to nothing. `resizable` adds a grab line on the edge that faces the page — a bottom rail's top edge.
`ShellDrawer` is the old name, an alias.

## Roles

`role` names the job and the rail takes its shell from it — side, tier, scrim and key — so an app stops choosing them
(RailRoles, 2026-10-25). `useRailKey(role, onToggle)` binds the key. Explicit props still win; no role draws an edge — `edge` is the opt-in. Every rail with a header draws the standard header: the title line with the ×, then the hairline (2026-10-25). Only `settings` dims the page.

| Role | Key | Desk | Phone (below 768) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `patch` | P | right, the app tier (380), no scrim | bottom, 70dvh |
| `page` | H | the settings rail's shell without the scrim — right, 380 (`SettingsPanel role="page"`) | fills the screen |
| `settings` | , | right, the app tier, the scrim (`SettingsPanel`) | fills the screen |
| `bottom` | E | bottom, 30dvh, resizable, no scrim, the full width over the nav rail with `RailNav` inside (`nav`) | bottom, 50dvh |
