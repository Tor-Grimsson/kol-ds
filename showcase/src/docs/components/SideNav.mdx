## Behavior

The app-site navigation rail, replicated from the elder reference
(kol-website apps/brand, 2026-08-09 user mandate). A category is a disclosure
button — icon, label, trailing caret — whose pages list opens in place; a
category with `to` and no `pages` (Home) renders as a plain link row.
Disclosure is independent per category and loads closed. The active category
is derived from the route — no resolver prop.

**Nested route groups (0.24.0).** A page row may be a `{ label, children }`
node: it renders as a non-routing group header over its own rows, indented one
step, recursively, and lights up when a leaf beneath it is the current route.
The tree shape is the opt-in — there is no prop — and a tree without group
nodes renders exactly as before. The earlier "two levels only" line was the
brand app's ruling about its scroll-spied `#anchor` sections (re-scoped
2026-08-26, sidenav-nested-groups); anchors stay dead, route groups do not.

**One control: the pill-marked grab edge** (2026-08-09 build order — the
chip Button is gone in both states). Click toggles expand↔collapse (a 3px
slop keeps toggle presses from jitter-resizing); drag resizes, snap-collapses
under the threshold, and snap-to-defaults on release near the stylesheet
width; keyboard on the focused separator: arrows resize, Home resets,
Enter/Space toggle. Everything drives `:root[data-sidenav="collapsed"]` and
the `kol-sidenav` storage schema.
Collapsed rows carry a DS [Tooltip](/components/popover-panel); the footer
wordmark swaps for its initial; the built-in
[ThemeToggle](/components/theme-toggle) falls back to its icon variant.
Distinct from the workshop's ShellSidebar (grouped doc trees).
