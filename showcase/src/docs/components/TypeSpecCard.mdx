<TypeSpecCard label='Heading 01' meta={[['Family', 'Sans'], ['Size', '48px']]}><TypeSample … /></TypeSpecCard>

## Behavior

The "data-sheet" member of the type-specimen kit: a two-column row pairing a
left meta panel of key/value pairs (font metrics) with a live sample slot on
the right, plus an optional corner label. Single column on mobile,
`240px + fluid` at lg. `meta` is arbitrary tuples — no fixed metric schema;
the sample is usually a [TypeSample](/components/type-sample) composed at the
call site.
