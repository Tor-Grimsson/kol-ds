```jsx
<ColorPickerPopover color={color} onChange={setColor} defaultValue="#E74C3C" />
```

## Behavior

Click the swatch: a popover right-aligned under it, flipping up where there is no room, with the
saturation square, the hue and opacity strips, and the hex. Every drag emits an uppercase hex — 8
digits only when opacity is under 100%. The hex field emits once it holds 6 or 8 digits. Esc and an
outside press close it; ⌥-click on the swatch resets to `defaultValue` instead of opening. None
(`transparent` or empty) draws the slashed swatch. A token or `currentColor` paints live in the
swatch and is resolved once when the picker opens. Built on [SpectrumControls](/components/spectrum-controls)
and its `AlphaStrip`.
