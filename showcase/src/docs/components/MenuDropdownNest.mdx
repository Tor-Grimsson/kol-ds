## Behavior

The nested-submenu member of the [MenuItem](/components/menu-item) family —
a row that holds a second-level list of
[MenuDropdownItem](/components/menu-dropdown-item) rows.

- **Accordion** (default) — a click expands the rows inline, under the row,
  indented on a hairline.
- **Flyout** (`flyout`) — the rows open in a panel **beside** the row, the way a
  desktop menu bar does. Hover opens, leaving closes, a click toggles (touch),
  → opens and ← closes. The panel stays on screen: it lifts above the window's
  foot and flips to the row's left at the right edge. Flyouts nest to any depth;
  `scroll` caps a long leaf list at 60vh.

A leaf click closes the whole menu in both modes.
