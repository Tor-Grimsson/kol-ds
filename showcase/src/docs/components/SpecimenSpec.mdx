## Behavior

The library master `SpecimenSpec` (Styleguide page; it was kol-foundry's `TypeSpecCard`, now an alias on the retirement ledger). The "data-sheet" member of the Specimen kit: a two-column row pairing a
left meta panel of key/value pairs (font metrics) with a live sample slot on
the right, plus an optional corner label. Single column on mobile,
`240px + fluid` at lg. `meta` is arbitrary tuples — no fixed metric schema;
the sample is usually a [SpecimenSample](/components/specimen-sample) composed at the
call site.
