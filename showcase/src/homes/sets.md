# Sets

A set is a **family** — every component one package ships, shown together, then the apparatus composed from them: the chess board with its notation and archive, the dashboard with its cards and charts, the foundry's specimen tools.

The same components also sit on the atomic ladder under Components. A set is the other way in: by who ships it and what they are for together.

| Is | Isn't |
|---|---|
| one package's family and what it composes | a page layout — that is a block |
| where a package's name leads | where a component is placed — that is its tier |
