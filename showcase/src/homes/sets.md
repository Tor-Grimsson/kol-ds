# Sets

A set is **the parts that make a thing**: the rack, the mixer, the blog, the foundry. Its page lists the parts first, by tier, then shows them working together. Examples are the chess board with its notation and archive, the dashboard with its cards and charts, and the website cards. A set can draw from more than one package. Cards takes the `Section*` family from kol-component and the content cards from kol-content.

The same components also sit on the atomic ladder under Components. A set is the other way in, by what they are for together. A family that is only one package's components is that package's page under Packages.

| Is | Isn't |
|---|---|
| the parts that make one thing, and the thing assembled | a page layout (that is a module) |
| free to cross packages | a package's own family (that is the package's page) |
