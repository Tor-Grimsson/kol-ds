# Sets

A set is a **family grouped by purpose**: components shown together, then what they compose. Examples are the chess board with its notation and archive, the dashboard with its cards and charts, and the website cards. A set can draw from more than one package. Cards takes the `Section*` family from kol-component and the content cards from kol-content.

The same components also sit on the atomic ladder under Components. A set is the other way in, by what they are for together. A family that is only one package's components is that package's page under Packages.

| Is | Isn't |
|---|---|
| a family grouped by purpose, and what it composes | a page layout (that is a block) |
| free to cross packages | a package's own family (that is the package's page) |
