# Molecules

A molecule is a **small assembly** — two or more parts you can name as KOL components, working as one unit: a label with its control, an input with its buttons, a trigger with its panel, a card, a row.

**The test: could you rebuild it from other KOL components?** If its parts are nameable — imported or hand-rolled — it is a molecule. A filter dropdown that draws its own trigger, panel and tags is a molecule even if it imports none of them.

Molecules nest atoms, never organisms. Imports go downward only: an atom never reaches up into a molecule.
