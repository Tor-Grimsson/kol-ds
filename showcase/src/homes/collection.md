# Collection

The [Library](/library) grouped by **belonging**. Nothing here is new: a collection groups what [Composition](/composition) already holds, by what it is for or by how it ships.

| Level | Is | Groups by |
|---|---|---|
| Sets | a family of components shown together, with what they compose | purpose |
| Packages | a published `@kolkrabbi/*` package: its family, its changelog, its version | shipping |

A set can draw from several packages: Cards takes the `Section*` family from kol-component and the content cards from kol-content. A package is what `npm install` gives you.

A set exists only when its purpose is worth showing apart from its package. A family that is just one package's components is that package's page.
