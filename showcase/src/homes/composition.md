# Composition

The [Library](/library) grouped by **size**. Each level is made of the one before it, so a component sits inside a block and a block sits inside an app.

| Level | Is | Made of |
|---|---|---|
| Components | one export with a face, placed on the atomic ladder as an atom, molecule or organism | styles |
| Blocks | shells and tools composed (sidenavs, headers, panels, toolbars), shown at every width to show how they breakpoint | components |
| Apps | a tool built on the design system, read through its layers: engine, shell, hub, tool | blocks and components |

Utilities sit beside the component ladder: they have no face of their own. The same things are grouped by belonging under [Collection](/collection).
