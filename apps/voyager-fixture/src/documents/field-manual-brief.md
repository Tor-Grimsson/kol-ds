# Field Manual 2027

A waterproof field manual for every Voyager team: routes, drills and kit lists in three parts, each keyed to a colour a guide can find in a storm. This brief sets the scope, the schedule and what each side brings.

## Scope

### 1. The manual

Three parts, printed on synthetic paper and spiral-bound so the book lies flat on a sledge.

|   |   |
|---|---|
| Format | A5, 112 pages, spiral |
| Paper | Synthetic, 200 µm, tear-proof |
| Parts | Routes · Drills · Kit |
| Print run | 240 copies |

### 2. The maps

Twelve route maps at 1:50 000, redrawn from the coastal series and checked on the ground.[^survey] ✋ Glacier routes to confirm with the guides.

### 3. The digital copy

The same manual as an offline web app for the team's phones, synced before every trip. 🅿️ Optional, quoted separately.

📍 Kickoff on 9 November in Reykjavík, at Grandagarður 8.

## Notes

### A. What Voyager brings

The route logs from the last three seasons, the current kit list, and two guides for the field test.

### B. What stays open

The colour of the third part, which the guides will choose after the field test.[^colour]

[^survey]: The coastal series was drawn in 2013–2016; four routes have changed since.
[^colour]: Orange and teal are on the table; red is ruled out because it reads as the rescue kit.
