Variants: from one lockup to a dozen, and the Rijksmuseum claim of "only black and white" is contradicted.

Cloudflare publishes stacked and horizontal lockups plus partner, corporate-program and team lockups. Vercel publishes black and white logotype and symbol only. Sanity's variants are code props (dark, three monogram schemes). The Rijksmuseum has one horizontal wordmark and a separate white-on-black "R" app mark. W+K's variants are found across the network: stacked, a one-line horizontal and a "W+K" monogram on the London site. The Rijksmuseum answer says the logo exists "only in black and white" (Monocle), but the Rijksmuseum design-rationale answer records that the 2013 Albert Heijn cartons show it white on a blue band, so treat "only black and white" as contradicted (see Data notes).

Sanity and Vercel pair a sans with a mono; Cloudflare, Rijksmuseum and W+K run one family. The lineage is Swiss grotesque: Univers (W+K), Akzidenz and Univers-like warmth (Waldenburg), and Basement's Geist reference list (Univers, SF Pro, Inter, Suisse International).

Hierarchy by size and tracking, not weight, is the shared choice of Vercel, Sanity and W+K.

Vercel: headings 400-450, display tracking -6% of size from 40px up; "Strong" is a 550-weight, darker-gray modifier, never colour. Sanity: every heading Waldenburg 400, tracking tightening from -0.01em at 20-24px to -0.04em at 50px and above; uppercase IBM Plex Mono for labels and buttons. W+K: body and every heading are Univers Light 300, "hierarchy by size" with no letter-spacing declared anywhere in its custom CSS. Cloudflare sits between: headings at weight 500 and -0.025em; its guide specifies semibold headlines at -3% and all-caps eyebrows at +5%. The Rijksmuseum is the outlier, using two weights (400 and 700) and a hard case contrast: every heading bold and uppercase, display line-height 0.9, and an unbounded fluid scale of calc(2.5rem + 5.625 * ((100vw - 20rem) / 100)) that reaches 103px at 1440px.
