Accessibility is rarely stated, and the most honest statement is a failing one.

The Rijksmuseum (found) publishes an unusually candid WCAG 2.2 AA statement (17 June 2025): "24 of the 55 success criteria … have been met", including text on images with too little contrast. Vercel (found) prefers APCA ("Prefer APCA over WCAG 2") alongside "Meet WCAG AA"; its primary text #171717 on #fff computes to 17.9:1. Sanity (partial) documents only Sanity Studio (WCAG 2.1 A and AA, 39 of 50 criteria, Aug 2022), not the brand; the researcher computed white on its orange at 3.19:1, so its CTAs use black text on orange (6.17:1). Cloudflare (found) has an accessibility section in its content guide and uses APCA in Kumo. W+K (partial) publishes nothing; computed pairs show #6c757d on its #e9ecef footer at 3.95:1, below AA for body text.

Sources: Cloudflare: Kumo accessibility · Rijksmuseum: Statement · Sanity: Accessibility · Vercel: Guidelines · W+K: Legal

Logo

Four of five logos are a wordmark with at most one extra element, and each wordmark is either drawn or set in a neo-grotesque.

Rijksmuseum: a pure wordmark, "RIJKS MUSEUM" in bold capitals drawn by Paul van der Laan, with a deliberate space and a Dutch IJ. W+K: a pure wordmark, "Wieden" over "Kennedy" in light Univers, with a thin plus hung off the right edge. Sanity: a drawn semi-serif wordmark plus a jagged "squiggle" symbol (Bleed), with a monogram on orange #FF5500. Vercel: a solid equilateral triangle (M57.5 0 115 100H0z) plus a Geist-like logotype; whether the logotype is custom or Geist Sans is unconfirmed (partial). Cloudflare: a two-part cloud (Tangerine #F6821F and Mango #FBAD41) with a flare streak and custom heavy rounded caps. Only Cloudflare's is multicoloured; Vercel's is mono only, with no colour logo.

Sources: Cloudflare: Logo · Rijksmuseum: Bold Monday · Sanity: sanityLogo.tsx · Vercel: Brands · W+K: Commons

Logo rules are published by one brand in full and one in part; the rest leave the rules unwritten.

Cloudflare (found): clearspace "no less than 100% of the height of the logomark", minimum 75px stacked and 100px horizontal for digital, 0.7in and 1in for print, plus a "What to avoid" list. Vercel (partial): clearspace equals the height of the triangle, symbol only where space is short, an 8-item misuse list. Rijksmuseum and Sanity are not-found. W+K (partial) has only observed practice: 180px wide on desktop, 100px at ≤991.98px, always single-colour; that is a description, not a rule. The tell: the two brands with the best published rules are the two with full brand guides.
