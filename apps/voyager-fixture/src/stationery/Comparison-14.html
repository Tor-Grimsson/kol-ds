Every brand shows inconsistency, and most of it sits where two surfaces meet.

Cloudflare (found): the guide says Inter, Tangerine #F6821F and black #000 logotype; the live site uses STK Bureau Sans, #ff5e1f and a #262626 nav logo. Rijksmuseum: styleguide names unused in CSS, --primary-color:#343b42 not in the guide, "Wit" typed "#FFFFFFF", and broken tokens shipped as [object Object]. Sanity: two blacks (#0D0E12 in the logo package, called "Placeholder color definitions until @sanity/color is updated with new branding", vs #0b0b0b), a dead #f50 declaration overridden by #ff560a, and different type on different surfaces (Waldenburg plus IBM Plex Mono on marketing, Inter on docs, JetBrains Mono on get-started). Vercel: "Agentic Infrastructure" against leftover "Frontend Cloud" copy, and rules in design.md that ban glows and marquees the flagship still uses. W+K: "© Wieden Kennedy 2026" without the plus, office tags "TOR" and "SYD" without it, and office counts that disagree (10, 9, 8).
