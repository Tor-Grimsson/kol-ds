Media

Media in a document: a grid of figures, a filmstrip for a run of images, a card on a link, and Quick Look over all of them. A click on any image opens it.

Figure grid

A run of lines that are each one embed renders as a grid. Every tile keeps a 4:3 frame and shows the whole image inside it, with its caption below.

In the markdown: one embed per line, ![[file|caption]] for repo files or ![caption](url) for bucket media. Video and PDF by extension.

Filmstrip

A tab group of images becomes a filmstrip: one fixed height, each image at its own width, dragged or paged with the chips. The caption is the tab label and the file type.

In the markdown: a :::tabs group with one image per ::tab Label.

Link card

Hovering a link opens a card under it after a short pause: a thumbnail and summary of where it goes. The image opens in Quick Look; the text follows the link.

Thumbnail and summary

The first lines of the page or section the link points to, cut at five lines.

example.com · Page title

The thumbnail keeps its own aspect between 1:1 and 2:1. On touch, a long press opens the card.
