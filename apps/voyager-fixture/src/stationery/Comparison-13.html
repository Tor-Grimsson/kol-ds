Icons are the one place most brands borrow: three of five use their own set, one uses a third-party library, and the agency draws one-offs.

Cloudflare: Untitled UI Icons (24x24, 2px stroke), verified by path geometry against the package. Sanity: @sanity/icons, 236 icons, 25x25 grid, 1.2px stroke, round joins. Vercel (partial): Geist icons, 16x16, a 1.5px outline stroke expanded to filled paths. Rijksmuseum: 32 custom symbols on mixed artboards, mostly filled. W+K: about six Sketch-exported SVGs with layer ids like "Page-1" still in the markup.

Three of the five signature devices are built from grids, dots or arcs.

Cloudflare and Sanity independently settled on near-identical dot patterns (a 0.75px-radius dot on a 12px tile for Cloudflare, 8px for Sanity).

Motion is quiet and switchable: Cloudflare's hero is an exact 8.0s loop (240 frames at 30 fps), Sanity's a 24.9s montage at 40% opacity, both off under prefers-reduced-motion; Vercel uses 150-300ms with a slight-overshoot easing, the Rijksmuseum mostly fades, W+K a 1.25s white page blocker (Cloudflare video, Sanity, Vercel, W+K).

The read

Each brand's distinctive choices are three or four decisions held for years.

Cloudflare: one orange as an area, the two-tone cloud and flare, schematic chrome. Rijksmuseum: a typographic name with a contested space and Dutch IJ, a logo as headline, a custom typeface. Sanity: one hot orange against black, a rough squiggle against a strict grid, a texture system. Vercel: the triangle as mark and object, an owned typeface, strict monochrome with a 10-step gray system. W+K: an agency site that refuses to sell, the plus, founder maxims as interface.
