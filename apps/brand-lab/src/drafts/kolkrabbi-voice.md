# Brand Voice Guide

Comprehensive guidelines for Kolkrabbi's brand voice, tone, and communication style.

---

## Overview

Kolkrabbi's voice is clear, structured, and crafted — mirroring the studio's design philosophy.

---

## Core Characteristics

- **Clear:** direct, unembellished, focused on meaning
- **Structured:** logical sequencing, clean phrasing, modular ideas
- **Quietly confident:** never loud, never salesy, always assured
- **Craft-driven:** precise language, well-chosen words
- **Human:** warm, but never casual or sloppy

---

## Tone Spectrum

| **Tone Angle** | **Level** | **Description** |
|---------------|-----------|-----------------|
| Professional | High | Polished, articulate, respectful |
| Technical | Medium | Uses design vocabulary when helpful |
| Artistic | Medium | Expressive but controlled |
| Humorous | Low | Dry, subtle, understated |
| Marketing | Very Low | Never hype-driven |

---

## Voice Dos

- Speak with calm authority
- Use structure and hierarchy
- Choose precise terminology
- Use short sentences when explaining systems
- Show the thinking, not just the outcome
- Keep metaphors minimal and sharp
- Use active voice

---

## Voice Don'ts

- No filler adjectives ("amazing", "outstanding", "world-class")
- No hype language
- No over-friendly casual tone
- No jargon without purpose
- No metaphors that over-explain

---

## Sentence Architecture

- Prefer short → medium → long cadence
- One idea per sentence
- Use clean transitions ("therefore", "as a result", "in practice")
- Use parallel structure for lists

---

## Design Vocabulary to Prefer

- Identity
- System
- Structure
- Logic
- Framework
- Foundation
- Modularity
- Grid
- Component
- Continuity
- Cohesion
- Clarity

---

## Design Vocabulary to Avoid

- "Digital magic"
- "Brand storytelling ecosystem vortex" style jargon
- Overly emotional language
- Trend-driven wording (except when discussing trends critically)
