# Review: Sanity dossier, strategy layer (`src/research/brands/sanity.json`)

Reviewed 2026-10-22. Scope: the seven `strategy` answers (brand-idea, audience, brief, design-rationale, campaigns, off-screen, perception), source types across the whole file, and assets under `public/research/sanity/strategy/`. The expression layer was reviewed and gated earlier (previous review kept in `_tmp/2026-10-22-reviews-before-strategy/sanity.md`). Checked against bleed.com, grafill.no, kampanje.com, kom24.no, cmscritic.com, cmswire.com, prnewswire.com, rebrand.gallery, fontsinuse.com, papier.work, sanity.io (blog, keynote, recap, careers) and everything.sanity.io.

## Verdict

**Needs fixes.** The quoting is unusually clean: 40+ quotes checked, all but one found verbatim at the cited URL, and the Norwegian sources (Kampanje, Grafill, Kom24) are used well. The problems are status inflation and labelling. `design-rationale` has no rationale from a named person and `perception` has no design-press critique, so both should be `partial`. One award year is wrong, one quote can't be found at any cited URL, two press releases are typed `press`, and none of the 14 strategy images are in `assetsIndex`.

- **design-rationale**: all rationale is unsigned Bleed prose. Grafill names the team (Papiernikova, Lia, Holstad Andersen), but the answer never names them, and nobody explains the two most visible choices: the **heavy serif wordmark** (see `bleed-wordmark-construction.png`), which sits apart from the Waldenburg/Plex type system, and the orange hue. The wordmark isn't mentioned at all. Per the ask, a rationale with no named source is `partial`.
- **perception**: the outside verdicts are award juries, an aggregator summary (Rebrand Gallery, descriptive) and a foundry's own Fonts In Use post. There is no design-press critique and no criticism. The ask leads with design-press critique and asks for "both praise and criticism". → `partial`.
- **brief**: what was wrong with the old triangle wordmark/red S is unstated (the gap says so). The business repositioning is well evidenced, so `found` holds.
- **audience**: what each audience should feel is given only once, jointly ("mission-critical stability and a limitless capacity for collaboration"). There's nothing per audience.
- **campaigns**: no paid campaign or campaign agency. Events carry the answer. The Community Event 2026 NY/LA posters and Café Sanity poster are listed as applications in the body but are mockups (see Asset problems). The body should say so; right now only the gap does.
- **off-screen**: every image is a presentation render. The only real-world placements (NYSE, Times Square, Pioneer Works) have no image. `found` is borderline; it holds on sanity.market and the event evidence.
