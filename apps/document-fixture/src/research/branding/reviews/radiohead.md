# Review — Radiohead branding dossier

Reviewed 2026-10-22 against `src/research/branding/brands/radiohead.json`. Pages fetched with curl and the Wayback Machine (WebSearch ran out after one query). Fetched copies are in `_tmp/2026-10-22-review-branding-radiohead/pages/`.

## Verdict

**Needs fixes.** The mapping is honest and useful. Each step says plainly when a band has no brief, guide, palette or tagline. It keeps "our reading" apart from what the makers said, and uses the makers' own accounts instead of inventing a system. The weak spot is citation. About five verbatim quotes are pinned to a page that doesn't contain them (mostly American Songwriter). One typeface claim has no source at all. The pitch takeaway says something no source supports. A few small facts are wrong (The Bends lettering, "mock-Esperanto", "61 cities"). The Guardian 2025 Donwood interview, which the researcher didn't read, closes the brief gap and adds to colour and pitch.

The three citizeninsane.eu copies checked against their originals are faithful (see below). Every quote taken from them is in the original.

## Wrong facts

1. **Quotes cited to American Songwriter that it doesn't contain.** The page paraphrases ("f-ed up phone call", "a long voice message"). The verbatim lines are elsewhere:
   - "Kid A was for me a fucked up phone call… really, really long answerphone message" and the minotaur "in tears" quote are verbatim in NME 2013 (Lucy Jones, "Stanley Donwood on the Stories Behind His Radiohead Album Covers"), Wayback: `https://web.archive.org/web/20130927150613/http://www.nme.com/blogs/nme-blogs/stanley-donwood-on-the-stories-behind-his-radiohead-album-covers`. They are also copied on Cultura Colectiva.
   - "hoping that in the morning the right cover and the right title would be obvious…" and "we took them all downstairs and used tape to stick them to the cupboards and the fridge" were found in no fetched source. Wikipedia (Kid A) paraphrases the story and quotes only "was obvious", citing Donwood's *There Will Be No Quiet* (2019), pp. 73–74. These two quotes are unverifiable and must not be shown as verbatim with this URL.
2. **The pitch takeaway and summary say Yorke rejects ideas in person** ("Yorke rejects ideas in person (Hail to the Thief topiary, AMSP Dalek)"; "turned down by Yorke"). No source says this. Flavorwire has Donwood: "I was embroiled in my topiary porn idea, but it quickly became apparent that another idea was required." Penguin calls it the "topiary cocks". The Dalek was dropped because they couldn't build it. Calling it "a hillside of giant topiary" also cleans up what the source says.
3. **The Bends lettering:** the summary says "red block capitals on The Bends". The sleeve has white capitals reversed out of a red band.
4. **The OK Computer booklet is real Esperanto, not "mock-Esperanto".** INJEKTILO and DANĜERA NAJBAR-AĴO are Esperanto, and Wikipedia (OK Computer) says "phrases in the constructed language Esperanto".
5. **"Handed out in 61 cities" (voice, touchpoints).** Print doesn't say this. Print says the paper went to CD and vinyl buyers. Wikipedia (The King of Limbs) says independent record shops worldwide, plus Donwood and Yorke handing it out at Rough Trade East. "61 cities" has no source.
6. **BD Plakatbau (type answer and `typefaces`).** The cited Made Good Designs page doesn't mention BD Plakatbau or any named Kid A face. It says the opposite: that any "exact Radiohead font" is guesswork. The claim has no source.
7. **"A symbol of looming danger and shattered expectations" (elements).** Donwood's own text (Slowly Downward print listing, Wayback `https://web.archive.org/web/20070610203044/http://shop.slowlydownward.com/Store/DisplayIndividualItem/1/575.html`) reads: "here I've coupled it with a scary bear; the record's iconic image, and a symbol of looming danger and shattered expectations". Grammatically the phrase describes the bear, not the pool. Wikipedia applies it to the pool image, and the dossier copies that. "Red swimming pool on Kid A's spine and disc" isn't supported by any fetched source.
8. **The A Moon Shaped Pool barn with a live audio feed** is cited to NME and Bonnefanten. Neither mentions it. It is in Wikipedia (A Moon Shaped Pool) and the Guardian 2025 interview ("a nearby building, with an audio link to the studio").
9. **The bear origin is not "Test Specimens (Far Out; Music Mascots Archive)".** Music Mascots Archive doesn't mention Test Specimens; only Far Out does.
10. **"No primary Donwood account of drawing the bear was found" (logo gap) is too strong.** Wikipedia (Kid A) cites Donwood's own book (*There Will Be No Quiet*, pp. 73–74) for the bedtime-story origin. The two stories don't conflict: Yorke's full Blender quote is "a deep paranoia of genetic engineering… And then from a children's book". It is one account told by two people.
11. **207,000 copies** is attributed to Clash in the positioning point but comes from Wikipedia (Kid A).
12. **Source titles are invented or garbled.** Rolling Stone 2021 appears under two titles, neither real. The Far Out bear piece is really "The true story behind Radiohead's bear symbol".

## Status problems

- **brief: `partial` should be `found`.** The Guardian 2025 interview (Alexis Petridis, 7 Aug 2025) gives the commission in Yorke's words, as Donwood tells it: "Our record sleeves are shit, do you want to come and have a go?" That covers who briefed whom, when, and why then. Per-album briefs stay a known gap in the points.
- **pitch: `found` stands only after the fixes.** Rejected routes are well documented by the maker: topiary, In Rainbows suburbia, the AMSP Dalek, and the King of Limbs Richter-style oils (Guardian 2025; Penguin). But the leading verbatim quote and the takeaway's Yorke claim must go. The In Rainbows release-model point is launch, not pitch.
- **reception: `found` is fine,** but it leans on Wikipedia for the Guardian verdict. Cite the review itself. The Times "defended it" is still second-hand (paywall).
- **motion: `found` is fair.** Examples, makers and dates are there, and the summary honestly says there are no principles and no sonic logo.
- **All the other `partial` statuses are honest.** Purpose, positioning, audience, personality, core-message, voice, logo, colour, type, elements and guide each say clearly that no formal artefact exists, and they separate "our reading". This is the right way to map a band onto a brand process.

## Closable gaps

- **Brief:** Guardian 2025 interview, `https://www.theguardian.com/artanddesign/2025/aug/07/stanley-donwood-interview-radiohead-thom-yorke-art-oxford`. It has the "record sleeves are shit" commission, the My Iron Lung hospital shoot that led to The Bends dummy still, and the working method (Donwood "moves into the studio… or a nearby building, with an audio link… Yorke then adds to the work").
- **Colour (Hail to the Thief):** same interview: LA signs "all in the same bold seven colours. So I bought those colours and started painting words." This is a maker's statement of the palette logic.
- **Pitch / insight (King of Limbs):** same interview: "My initial idea… was blurred photorealist paintings in oil, like Gerhard Richter, but it went very badly. Then Radiohead did a playthrough in this beautiful old barn…" This is a rejected route plus its replacement.
- **Imagery (Kid A, OK Computer):** same interview: Kid A canvases painted in a rented studio in Bath, with grit and Artex. OK Computer used a tablet whose stylus acted as an eraser, "so we wanted the mistakes to still be there".
- **Reception and agency:** Guardian review (Eddy Frankel, 29 Jul 2025, two stars), `https://www.theguardian.com/artanddesign/2025/jul/29/stanley-donwood-thom-yorke-this-is-what-you-get-review-radiohead-ashmolean-museum`: "Nothing is farmed out to designers or agencies – Radiohead's visual identity has been fully overseen by Donwood and Yorke." This is independent support for `brand.agency` and the guide answer.
- **Elements / logo:** Donwood's own Slowly Downward text (Wayback URL above) is primary for the pool and bear pairing.
- **Mood / personality:** NME 2013 original (Wayback URL above) is primary for the answerphone and minotaur quotes.
- **Ashmolean:** `ashmolean.org/node/4585066` still returns a maintenance page. Not closable today.
- **BD Plakatbau / Kid A typeface:** not closed. Fonts In Use wasn't reachable without search. Drop the claim rather than keep it marked unconfirmed.
