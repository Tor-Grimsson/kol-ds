## Verdict

**Needs fixes.** The quotes the dossier uses mostly check out, and the core facts (studio, typeface, launch date, the 220-page manual, the D&AD pencil) hold. But the researcher could not reach the studio's own texts, and both are on the Wayback Machine:

- **"Whitney Graphic Identity"** (EJ essay, May 2013): `http://web.archive.org/web/20170217064852/http://www.jetset.nl/archive/whitney-museum-identity`
- **"The Whitney Tapes"** (EJ's collected 2013–14 interviews, posted May 2023, including Creative Review, D&AD, YCN, Entrepreneur and Gizmodo): `http://web.archive.org/web/20230528184342/http://jetset.nl/archive/the-whitney-tapes`

Together they close or substantially fill 8 of the 12 partial answers. They also show four claims are wrong: how the studio was chosen, "research was internal", who the PRINT award went to, and the Hyperallergic date. Many studio quotes are cited through critics (Hyperallergic, Laranjo) when the primary source is now available.

The typeface is confirmed as **Neue Haas Grotesk**, not Unica. EJ: "this word should always be set in Neue Haas Grotesk (NHG)". EJ also confirmed that the Whitney's line and type are black on white. The palette hexes are still nominal, and the grey hex is made up.

## Wrong facts

1. **How the studio was chosen (pitch, takeaway and point; brief).** "The museum chose the studio after a two-year internal review" is wrong. Here is the process according to EJ (Tapes, YCN 17 June 2013):
   - the in-house design department listed 20+ studios
   - about 12 were interviewed on existing work
   - a shortlist of four went to a **paid pitch**, with EJ the only one without a US office
   - the phone call came in **fall 2011**
   - Rebecca Gimenez, then head of graphic design, sent the first email in spring 2011

   EJ worked on it November 2011 to May 2013.
2. **"Research was internal" / "no audience research is on record" (insight).** Wrong. The brief was "an enormous cardboard box, containing dozens of documents and files, samples of all the previous graphic identities, detailed research reports and questionnaires, books on the history of the Whitney" (Tapes, Entrepreneur Q5). There were also "positioning documents" (EJ essay).
3. **PRINT award attributed to Entro (reception, point 6).** PRINT's 2016 Best of Region NYC page never names Entro. Entro's own award on record is the **SEGD 2016 Merit**.
4. **Hyperallergic date.** The article is 21 May 2013, not 22 May, in every source entry and in reception. The launch point ("Hyperallergic, the next day, called it 'unveiled today'") is wrong: it was the same day.
5. **D&AD credits (reception point 1, motion point 2).** "Integrated Graphics", "credited to Experimental Jetset and the Whitney in-house team" and "Digital Design Agency: Linked By Air" are not on the cited page. Restate it as: Yellow Pencil 2014, Graphic Design, nomination transferred by EJ to the in-house team (cite the Tapes).
6. **Personality words presented as general brand traits (personality, mood).** EJ used "industrial directness", "low-fi/low-tech casualness", "freshness" and "openness" about **type applied directly to the wall in the sign system**, not about the brand as a whole. They are cited through Laranjo when EJ's own essay is the source.
7. **Membership copy (voice example and point).** "ACCESS" is missing (see table row 19).
8. **Palette grey `#808080` (palette).** No source gives a grey value. Entro only names "grey". The launch print does use grey type tints (dezeen-8 membership card), but no value was published.
9. **"Animated GIF" (core-message, motion, assetsIndex).** `dezeen-1-anim.gif` is a single static frame.
