## Wrong facts

1. **"Mailchimp had never taken venture money" — attributed to Fast Company (brief, point 5).** The Fast Company article contains no such statement; "venture" appears only in its footer. The fact is true but needs a source that says it (e.g. Wikipedia or the Intuit acquisition coverage).
2. **"The Drum: R/GA brought the brand to life using brand guidelines from COLLINS" (guide, point 4).** The Drum's article contains no mention of guidelines. Unsupported.
3. **"The Drum (from the COLLINS statement): … evolving early whimsy 'into more purposeful, playful surrealism'" (personality, point 4).** The Drum ties only "unconventionality" to Collins. The surrealism sentence is about the system "expertly expressed by the Mailchimp design team and its creative agency R/GA". This looks like launch press-release language, not a COLLINS statement.
4. **"Fonts In Use summed it up: 'Mailchimp demonstrated to entrepreneurs that leveling up your business doesn't mean erasing your peculiarities'" (core-message, point 4).** Fonts In Use introduces this with "As Collins put it:". It is a COLLINS quote reported by Fonts In Use.
5. **Brief point 2 puts "R/GA rebuilt the website and customer experience" inside a COLLINS-attributed bullet.** The COLLINS page only lists R/GA in the credits. The website role comes from The Drum ("R/GA assisted in the user experience, building a new site architecture").
6. **Launch date "26–27 Sep 2018".** Chestnut's tweet is dated 26 Sep ("Later today, we're launching"), and Brand New wrote on 27 Sep that "Yesterday, Mailchimp introduced a new identity". The launch was **26 Sep 2018** (US time). The press stories and the announcement post are dated 27 Sep.
7. **"Design Week readers objected" (reception).** It was one reader comment ("EMLD", 4 Oct 2018), answered by Tom Banks on 5 Oct, after which the illustrators were named. The article is dated 1 Oct 2018, not just "Oct 2018".
8. **Brand New review "paywalled; its verdict could not be read" (reception).** Wrong. See Closable gaps.
9. **Brief takeaway "35 new products weren't selling".** This overstates COLLINS, which says "the revenue dial didn't move much". Also note that "AI-driven" describing 2018 products is COLLINS's present-day rhetoric, so keep it in quotes.

## Closable gaps

- **Brand New "Going Bananas" (Armin Vit, 27 Sep 2018) is readable via Wayback:** https://web.archive.org/web/20190322115625/https://www.underconsideration.com/brandnew/archives/new_logo_and_identity_for_mailchimp_by_collins_and_in_house.php (note the URL slug ends `_by_collins_and_in_house.php`). Verbatim points:
  - Disclaimer: "Mailchimp has been a sponsor of our Brand New Conference since 2010 … My opinion is definitely influenced by this."
  - Verdict: "Overall, though, this is a fantastic update that maintains the weirdness of the company but delivers it in a new grown up style that feels authentic, unique, and bananas."
  - Colour: yellow is "such a non-web-platform color, where they all lean to blues and greens". This is an independent source for the positioning "our reading" about yellow vs a blue category.
  - Criticism: illustrations "sometimes they feel TOO weird and ambiguous"; photography "starts to get too artsy and interpretative in a pretentious way"; he "would have liked to see the 'm' uppercased".
  - Logo history: Freddie and the wordmark were locked up together "way back in 2001 … by Mailchimp's co-founder, Ben Chestnut". Freddie lost fur and the "M" on the cap, with a "cleaner ear, simpler hairline".
  - Type: Cooper Light builds "on the rising trend of slightly ironic type choices in part made popular by the Chobani redesign".
- **ADC Awards 2019 Silver Cube:** https://www.oneclub.org/awards/adcawards/-award/32433/ — "Mailchimp Customer Experience", Craft in Online / Mobile / Art Direction – Single. Agencies credited: R/GA (New York) and COLLINS (New York). Design firms: **Animade** and **Giant Ant**. This adds an award to reception and outside animation studios to motion.
- **Cannes Lions:** lovethework.com lists "Mailchimp Rebrand" (…/mailchimp-rebrand-599252) and "Mailchimp Customer Experience" (…/597733) entries, but the pages render no award level. Unverified; do not claim a Lion.
- **design.mailchimp.com is the primary source** for "We anchor on a single color, used with purpose…" (and for "a devotion to craft"). Cite it directly instead of Truly Deeply.
