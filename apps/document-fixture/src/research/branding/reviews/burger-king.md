# Review — Burger King (2021 rebrand, JKR)

Reviewed 2026-10-22 against `src/research/branding/brands/burger-king.json`.

## Verdict

**Needs fixes.** The dossier is well built and mostly accurate: almost every podcast quote is verbatim and correctly attributed, the release and award facts hold up, and all 22 assets are correct. What needs fixing is narrower: one paraphrase presented as a quote, quotes cited to pages that don't contain them, the stock-figure conflict (now resolved), D&AD levels (now confirmed), and `mood` listing a logo descriptor.

## Wrong facts

1. `imagery` point 6: "Ads of the World (JKR): illustration says \"big flavor doesn't have to be serious\"". The page reads "the illustrative technique shows that big flavor isn't always serious." This is a paraphrase presented as a quote.
2. `purpose` point 2: the release text reads "will more authentically represent Burger King's values", not "Burger King values".
3. `mood` array and the `mood` example: "confident, simple and fun" is the release's description of **the logo**, not the brand's mood.
4. `type` example "Flame Regular · Flame Bold · Flame Sans" cites Creative Bloq, which does not name them.
5. `insight` example / point 7: the Smith "negative perceptions… synthetic, artificial and cheap" quote is cited to Creative Bloq 2026, which itself attributes it to an unnamed 2021 Dezeen piece. The 9 Jan 2021 Dezeen article does not contain it.
6. `brief` summary: "briefed… through RBI design head Rapha Abreu". No source says Abreu did the briefing. Creative Review says "Burger King originally approached JKR"; the podcast doesn't say who briefed.
7. `reception` summary: "the stock-jump figure differs between JKR (7.8%) and the entry text (5.1%)". Both are real and measure different windows (see notes).
8. `reception` point 7: "pencil level not confirmed". Logos = Wood Pencil; Uniform = Shortlist.
