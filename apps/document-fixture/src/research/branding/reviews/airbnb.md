# Review — Airbnb branding dossier

Reviewed 2026-10-22 against `src/research/branding/brands/airbnb.json`. Pages were fetched live where possible and through the Wayback Machine otherwise. Fetched copies are in `_tmp/2026-10-22-review-branding-airbnb/pages/`. WebSearch ran out after one query, so gap-closing used direct fetches only.

## Verdict

**Needs fixes.** The core of the dossier holds up. Every quote checked from Chesky's post, the DesignStudio Brand/Process/Digital pages, Further, Brand New, Dezeen, the Cereal post, It's Nice That, Core77 and Logo Design Love is verbatim and correctly attributed. The problems are narrower:

- One source is counted twice, under a title that doesn't exist.
- One narrated sentence is presented as a quote.
- Lottie credits are misspelled and unsourced.
- The colour answer misses a first-party hex.
- The Rausch and #FF385C values rest on website-derived reference sites.
- A few "our reading" labels are on the wrong lines.

## Wrong facts

1. **elements, example "Teo Connor on 2025 3D work".** "The app design, user interface, 3D and illustrations were all done by an in-house team" is It's Nice That's narration, framed "…Teo tells us". It is a paraphrase presented as a quote.
2. **motion, the Lottie point.**
   - The animator is **Salih Abdul-Kareem** (School of Motion), not "Abdul-Karim".
   - The cited page names only him and Brandon Withrow. Gabriel Peal, Leland Richardson and the year "2017" are not in it.
   - "Lead animator" is not the cited title; School of Motion calls him a motion designer.
3. **colour, point "Later product red #FF385C is documented only by unofficial design-system reconstructions; no announcement found".** This is wrong. Airbnb's own newsroom favicon (`https://news.airbnb.com/icon.svg`) is the Bélo with `fill="#FF385C"`. That is a first-party file carrying the current Rausch.
4. **Dezeen double-count.**
   - `https://www.dezeen.com/?p=498266` and `?p=497898` resolve to the **same** article: canonical `https://www.dezeen.com/2014/07/16/airbnb-rebrand-designstudio-logo-belo/`, titled "DesignStudio creates new logo for Airbnb".
   - The title "Airbnb rebrand by DesignStudio aims for visual consistency" does not exist.
   - Distinct sources drop from 30 to 29. That still passes the 20+ rule.
5. **brief, the "Scope (Dezeen)" point.** "DesignStudio then worked in-house on iOS, Android and web" comes from the DS Digital page ("we worked in-house…"), not from Dezeen.
6. **voice, point "Our reading: Brand New judged the launch language…".** This is Armin Vit's verdict, not "our reading", and it belongs in reception rather than voice.
7. **insight, the Further point.**
   - It is labelled "paraphrased by our reading", but "Belonging the world over was Airbnb's truth and its differentiator" is verbatim on Further.
   - Further also gives the insight outright: "Airbnb is all about people and not about the places at all." That quote is missing.
   - Further says the immersion covered "18 metro cities" while DS Process says 13 cities. The conflict is unflagged.
8. **logo, the Automation Anywhere point.** "Automation Anywhere changed its mark" has no source in the answer.

## Closable gaps

- **colour (official hex):** `https://news.airbnb.com/icon.svg` has `fill="#FF385C"` (first-party, current). The newsroom brand asset `https://news.airbnb.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2020/03/BeloRauschNewsroomFeatured_200316.png` confirms "Rausch" is still the name of the 2020+ colour. It is a gradient, so it gives no single hex. No first-party source for #FF5A5F was found.
- **voice:** cite the pinned snapshot `https://web.archive.org/web/20160614053505/http://www.freshconsulting.com/uiux-principle-22-writing-is-a-key-ingredient-in-ux/` and name the chain (Ben Spencer, Fresh, 15 Apr 2016 → Ariel Schwartz, Business Insider → Schleifer). The BI original is still not found; a search was not possible this session.
- **personality (lead):** the Cereal press ties the name to "a core value to think about unexpected methods of problem-solving" (Saarinen in AIGA; Airbnb in Dezeen 2018). That points to Airbnb's company core values, possibly including "Be a cereal entrepreneur", which would give the personality answer a stated value set. careers.airbnb.com doesn't list them; a dated source is needed before using them.
- **positioning / reception:** the WARC stub confirms the **2017 Effie Silver** (TBWA\Chiat\Day, Starcom), which can be cited as an award. The 17% / Booking.com figures stay paywalled.
- **pitch, motion, guide, imagery, mood, audience:** nothing new found by direct fetch. The gaps stand as written.
