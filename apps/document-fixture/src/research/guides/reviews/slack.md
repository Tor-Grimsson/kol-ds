# Review: Slack Brand Guidelines (`guides/slack.json`)

## Verdict

**Needs fixes.** The chapter list and almost every number are right. The faults are four wrong statements (brand values, clear space, page counts, the voice "pairs") plus one gap that can be closed from captures already on the Wayback Machine. Nothing is broken structurally and no asset is broken.

Checked against: the September 2020 PDF (50 pp, read in full as text and rendered), the 2019 PDF (60 pp), the Wayback brand-center captures (Jan 2023 to 2026), the live media kit and Brand Terms page, and every press URL.

## Chapters check

All 33 `chapters` match the printed contents page (p3): same titles, same order, same page numbers, every `source` anchor lands on the right page. Section 1 has 5 entries, Section 2 has 21, Section 3 has 7. Nothing missing, nothing invented. The `type` tags are right (Video as `motion`, Brand architecture as `logo`, Merchandise as `governance` are defensible).

Two things are off:

- **`size` counts illustration pages as content.** Pages 4, 7, 9, 12, 23, 30, 36 and 42 are full-bleed illustrations with no text. So "Logo misuse, 2 pages" is one page plus an illustration (p22, p23), "Type specimen, 2 pages" is one page plus an illustration (p29, p30), and "Partnership lockup, 2 pages" is one page plus an illustration (p35, p36).
- **The device is never mentioned.** Full-bleed illustrations as page breaks are a structural feature of this guide, and the `structure` answer does not say so.

## Wrong facts

1. **Brand values: "each with three keywords" is wrong** (chapter 3 `note`, `story` points). p10 gives Empathy 3, Courtesy 3, Solidarity 3, but Craftsmanship 4, Playfulness 4 and Thriving 2. The text under each is a short paragraph, not "a line".
2. **Clear space is "not stated on the logo pages" / "absent from pp17-23" is wrong** (`logo` summary and takeaway, `omissions` points). p18 draws clear space around both the horizontal and stacked logo (grey octothorpe and lozenge ghosts), and p35 states in words "Clear space equals the width of one octothorpe" for the partnership lockup. What is true: the unit for the standalone logos is written down only on p44, and the p18 diagram carries no caption.
3. **Page counts contradict each other.** `structure` says logo pages "6 (17-23)" and lockups "4 (32-36)". `logo` says "seven pages". 17-23 is seven pages but p23 is an illustration, so the logo pages are 17-22 (six); 32-36 is five pages and p36 is an illustration, so four lockup pages.
4. **Voice: "seven are/aren't pairs" is wrong** (`voice` summary). The p14 list has seven traits. Five carry a "never/but/not" counterweight; Intelligent and "Clear, concise and human" do not.
5. **Co-branding "legally required" quote is cited to the wrong page** (`governance` points). It is on `/brand-architecture`; `/cobranding` has the "Use this logo where data sharing occurs" wording. Add the right URL as a governance source.
6. **2019 `[confirm this list]` example cites `#page=53`; it is `#page=52`** (`omissions` examples).
7. **Brand center timeline is late by six months.** The "under construction" notice is already in the Wayback capture of 11 Sep 2024 (and in every one since), not only "by March 2025". The `format` summary can say "from September 2024".
8. **`structure`: "strategy and legal take roughly a quarter each"** is 11 and 10 of 50 pages, about a fifth each.
9. **`type` omission "no download route for the fonts"** holds for the PDF only. The Jan 2023 brand-center typography page has "Download Larsseit", "Download Slack Circular" and "Download Tazugane" buttons (targets not captured). Say so.
10. **`governance` distribution is stale.** The live Slack Brand Terms (slack.com/terms-of-service/slack-brand, linked from the media kit) now say "Copyright 2023 Slack Technologies, LLC", point logo downloads to a Salesforce Widen collection (not Brandfolder) and give "one pill shape" for both logos. The dossier's "Brandfolder still lists a 22-asset collection" is true but is no longer the official route.
11. **`guide.maker`** names the identity team (Slack in-house + Pentagram, 2019). The guide itself credits nobody, so the field mixes the logo's makers with the document's. Keep, but say "identity by..., guide uncredited".

## Status problems

- `applications` stays `partial`, but its gap text is false: it says the brand-center events, swag and presentation pages were "not captured or login-gated". They are captured and readable (see Closable gaps). The PDF itself is still thin; keep the status, fix the gap and the summary.
- `found` is earned for format, structure, rule-style, story, voice, logo, colour, type, governance. `strengths` is `found` on two vendor blogs and one line each; acceptable, but see Source-type problems.
- `imagery`, `elements`, `motion`, `omissions` as `partial` are right.
- `type` is `found` although fonts' source and licensing are missing; fine, the gap is stated in the summary.

## Website-not-guide problems

None serious. Brand-center content is labelled "brand center" wherever used, and the media kit and Brand Terms are used only for distribution, contact and dating. The `guide.url` is a third-party Shopify mirror because slack.com's own URL is dead and was never archived; the document is internally consistent (contents, footers, metadata) and the dossier says it is a mirror.

## Closable gaps

1. **`applications` (partial): four pages are on the Wayback Machine and readable:**
   - `web.archive.org/web/20240223230253/https://brand.slackhq.com/presentations`: the Slack deck is "a version of the Salesforce corporate template"; display font Avant Garde Demi SFDC, body Salesforce Sans; Slack/Salesforce paired lockup on content pages, "Slack from Salesforce" on cover and thank-you slides. This contradicts the PDF p27 note that decks use Larsseit.
   - `web.archive.org/web/20240224002651/https://brand.slackhq.com/swag`: swag principles (high quality, useful, memorable), swag store, preferred vendor, "Please don't brand food, beverage or bathroom products."
   - `web.archive.org/web/20230128120321/https://brand.slackhq.com/events-slack` and `/events-salesforce`: logo placement at registration and demo stations, Salesforce leads at Salesforce events.
   Still not captured: CMS, Email, Spacesuit, Video, Sound (checked the Wayback index for the whole host).
2. **`motion` gap:** the "Sound page not captured" claim is correct (no capture exists). Video also has no capture. Nothing to close.
3. **`elements`:** gap stands. The brand center has no layout grid either.
4. **`omissions` clear-space point** is closable by the Brand Terms page (live) plus p18 and p35 as above.

## Asset problems

All 12 images exist, open and show the page they say they show (p3, 17, 18, 22, 24, 25, 26, 29, 35, 37, 39, 44). All are indexed; none are unindexed. Labels are right; the p18 label ("construction/clear-space ghosts") is accurate and is the evidence against the dossier's own clear-space claim.

Weak spots:

- No image for p19 (small logo, the pixel numbers the takeaway leads with), p33 (lockup spec) or p14 (voice pairs). `voice`, `story`, `motion` and `strengths` carry no image.
- `rule-style` uses p22 and p18, which is right.

## Source-type problems

- **Phase 3** (phase3mc.com) and **ManyPixels** are agency and vendor content-marketing blogs. Typing them `press` is generous; they are commentary, not independent coverage. Fine as the only outside voices if the summary says how thin they are (it does).
- **Alice Lee** (`agency`): a freelance illustrator who made the illustration voice, not the guide. Acceptable as maker of the illustration style; `byalicelee.com` is her own case study.
- Third-party hosted copies of Slack's own documents (Shopify CDN, anthedesign.fr) are typed `owned`; correct for the document, titles already say "mirror".
- The `strengths` example "Slack publishes a downloadable PDF through its media kit" is quoted as ManyPixels' praise but is a description, not praise. Use the "specific and slightly funny / unusually blunt" lines instead.

## MUST-FIX

1. `chapters[2]` note and `story` points: replace "three keywords and a line" with the real counts (Empathy 3, Courtesy 3, Craftsmanship 4, Playfulness 4, Solidarity 3, Thriving 2) and call the text a short paragraph.
2. `logo`, `omissions`: remove "clear space is not stated on the logo pages / absent from pp17-23". State: drawn (unlabelled) on p18, "equal to the width of one octothorpe" on p35 for the partnership lockup, unit for standalone logos written only on p44. Retitle the `logo` takeaway to match.
3. `structure`, `logo`: make page counts agree (logo content pp17-22 = 6 pages, lockups pp32-35 = 4, plus illustration pages 23, 30, 36). Add a point that illustration pages (4, 7, 9, 12, 23, 30, 36, 42) work as full-bleed breaks. Fix the `size` of chapters 11, 17 and 22 to "1 page + illustration" or equivalent. Change "about a quarter each" to "about a fifth each".
4. `applications`: rewrite the gap and add points from the captured presentations (Salesforce template, Avant Garde Demi SFDC and Salesforce Sans, paired lockup), swag, events-slack and events-salesforce pages (URLs under Closable gaps); add them as `owned` sources. Keep `partial`.
5. `voice`: change "seven are/aren't pairs" to seven traits, five with a "never/but/not" counterweight (summary and takeaway wording).
6. `governance`: cite the "legally required" quote to `/brand-architecture`; update distribution to the live Brand Terms (Widen link, 2023 copyright line, "one pill shape" for both logos) and note Brandfolder as the older route.
7. `omissions`: change the 2019 "[confirm this list]" example source to `#page=52`.
8. `type`: where the omission says "no download route", add that the Jan 2023 brand-center page has Download Larsseit / Slack Circular / Tazugane buttons (targets not captured).
9. `format`/`guide.edition`: say the brand-center notice appears from Sept 2024, mark "2019 edition" as dated by content (copyright line, no date in the file; mirror file created July 2025), and make `guide.maker` say the guide itself is uncredited.
