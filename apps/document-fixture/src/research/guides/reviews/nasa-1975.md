# Review: nasa-1975 (NASA Graphics Standards Manual, 1975/76)

Checked against NASA's 60-page PDF (text extracted page by page, pages rendered and viewed) and the cited press pages.

## Verdict

**Solid.** The chapters, quotes, page numbers and measured values are accurate. Two claims are wrong (dummy copy, no contractor or approval rules). Several gaps close from the guide. Source typing is inconsistent in three places.

## Chapters check

- Ten top-level entries (Introduction, 1 to 9) match the index on PDF pp.2-3 verbatim, in order. None missing, none invented. Source page anchors all land on the right page (p.2, 5, 14, 19, 24, 25, 45, 47, 58, 59).
- Sizes: all are index page counts, which is right, but the scan itself differs. Vehicles run 11 scan pages (pp.47-57), not 8. Only Reproduction Art notes the scan difference.
- Signage: the guide says "exterior (6.1) and interior (6.2)" (p.45), but the index lists only 6.1. "1 gatefold section (6.1) plus continued principles" is acceptable, but the 6.2 reference is not recorded.
- Types: Ch.1 is `logo`, but 1.3 to 1.5 (3 of 9 pages) is the colour spec. Ch.5 is `layout`, but 5.3 to 5.6 is four typography pages. Cross-guide comparison will find no NASA colour or type chapter. Ch.4 Forms is `templates`, but it is a transitional "temporary measure" with no template. Ch.3 is `templates` and Ch.7/8/9 are `applications` for the same kind of material, so the split is arbitrary. Judgement calls, not errors.

## Wrong facts

1. `voice` summary: "All dummy copy is Latin or nonsense text, so no model copy is given." False. News Releases (3.5, p.23) carry real English sample copy ("Skylab puts out welcome mat for comet", dateline and release-number format). 5.3 to 5.6 set a real Ruskin passage as the type specimen. Only the letterhead, press-kit and cover pages use lorem ipsum or nonsense.
2. `governance`, `omissions` and `rule-style`: "almost every page" carries the Graphics Coordinator line. Overclaim, see row 34.
3. `governance` takeaway: "One named role... controls advice, artwork and supplies." Two more control points are in the guide: new aircraft paint schemes "must be submitted to the Aircraft Office for Headquarters approval" and "detailed specification drawings... are available from the NASA Aircraft Office" (7.8, pp.54-55). Vinylcals and decals come from HQ, but "Decals are not available at NASA Headquarters because of limited requirements" (9.1).
4. `omissions` and `governance`: "No co-branding rules for contractors or partners; only seal and mission-patch separation." Overstated. 6.1 (p.45) has a contractor-sign model ("c. Contractor sign. Possibly modular so that the bottom portion can be replaced.", shown as "Alpha Omega Corporation" under a NASA red header). 7.8 (p.55) gives a rule for leased or loaned aircraft ("a minimum marking of a red logotype with black Helvetica Medium numerals").
5. `colour` summary lists allowed combinations as "red only on white or light neutrals, white on dark, black or white on mid-tones." It omits two stated rules: white logotype on a NASA red background (p.8), and red only "when a second color is available" (p.7).

## Status problems

- `colour` is `found` although the ask is values (HEX, RGB, CMYK, Pantone) and the guide gives none. The answer says so, and the Federal Standard numbers and process recipe are real specs, so `found` is defensible. If the standard is "values given", it is `partial`. Left to the owner.
- `governance` `partial` is right, but the gap text must change (see wrong facts 3 and 4).
- `imagery` `partial` is right. The gap says "no... cropping/treatment rules" and "checked... interior grids 5.14-5.20", but those pages hold photo-placement rules (below).
- `story`, `voice`, `motion`, `omissions`: statuses earned. `motion` is `not-found` with an empty summary, which the validator allows.
- `strengths` `found` is earned: three independent press pieces plus the maker.
- `voice` gap says "Checked every page... especially 3.3, 3.5", yet misread 3.5 (wrong facts 1).

## Website-not-guide problems

- `logo` lists "NASA Brand Center - Brand Guidelines (current Logotype rules)" as a source, but nothing in the logo answer uses it. Drop it.
- `governance` leans on the Brand Center for a point ("Today's rule", "afterlife") and cites three Brand Center or Brand Guidelines URLs. It is labelled as later history, which is acceptable, but it is not the 1976 guide. Keep one example at most, clearly marked.
- No CSS or marketing-site leakage otherwise. All rules and values come from the PDF.

## Closable gaps

- `governance` gap ("no approval process"): partly closable. Aircraft Office approval (7.8), Graphics Coordinator approval for special vehicles (7.1) and stem-words (5.1), and the "officially in effect" index are all present. Add them. Still true: no named person, change log or font distribution.
- `governance` / `omissions` co-branding: close from 6.1 contractor sign and 7.8 leased aircraft.
- `voice` gap: add the news-release sample copy and date/release-number conventions (3.5, p.23) as the guide's only model copy. The gap itself (no principles, word lists) remains valid.
- `imagery` gap: add photo-placement and caption rules from the grids: "Captions are positioned under photographs" (5.17, p.41), "Photographs always positioned at top or bottom of page" (5.18, p.42), "Captions for all images are grouped in one place" (5.19, p.43), and "Special attention must be paid to the quality of the photograph" with 4-colour process "desirable" (5.10, p.34). No library or style, still true.
- `omissions`: its claim "no voice" and "no co-branding" must be adjusted as above. All other claimed omissions check out: no numeric colour values, no general minimum size, clear space only for decals, forms deferred, no motion or screen rules.

## Asset problems

- All 12 files exist, open, are readable and are all in `assetsIndex`. Page labels match: p01 title, p02 (i) index sections 1-5, p05 = 1.1, p08 = 1.4, p09 = 1.5, p10 = 1.6, p11 = 1.7, p27 = 5.3, p38 = 5.14, p45 = 6.1, p50 = 7.4, p59 = 9.1.
- Raw scans: black bars on the right and bottom and visible punch holes on most pages. Legible but untidy. Crop if the page design allows.
- `elements` has one asset and `type` only the Helvetica page. No asset shows 5.4 to 5.6, the 7.1 gray scale or the 3.1 letterhead, though the answers quote them. Optional.

## Source-type problems

1. The NASA "The NASA Worm" podcast is `owned` in `story` and `agency` in `strengths`. Pick one. Recommend `agency`, since the value is Danne's account as maker.
2. standardsmanual.com product pages (`format`, `motion`, `applications` "The Worm") are typed `press`. They are the reissue publisher's shop pages, not independent coverage. The `applications` "The Worm" page is cited for nothing in the text. Retype as `reference` or drop it.
3. Creative Review is cited in `omissions` but its content is not used (the gap says no critic names the gaps). Drop or use it.

## MUST-FIX

1. `voice` summary: delete "All dummy copy is Latin or nonsense text, so no model copy is given." Replace with: the only model copy is the news-release sample (3.5, p.23) plus the Ruskin type specimen; everything else is placeholder. Add the 3.5 example to `examples`.
2. Replace "on almost every page" with "on about a quarter of the pages" (or list the pages: 1.7, 1.8, 1.9, 2.1, 2.2 to 2.5, 3.1, 3.2, 5.1, 5.2, 7.1) in `rule-style` points, `governance` takeaway/points and anywhere else it appears.
3. `governance`: add the Aircraft Office approval and specification-drawings route (7.8, p.54-55, "must be submitted to the Aircraft Office for Headquarters approval"). Change the takeaway from "one named role" to two roles, and amend the gap.
4. `omissions` (point 6) and `governance` (separation-rules point): replace "No co-branding rules for contractors or partners" with the contractor-sign model (6.1, p.45) and the leased/loaned-aircraft minimum marking (7.8, p.55). State that there is no general partner policy.
5. `colour` summary: add "white on NASA red" (1.4, p.8) and "NASA red only when a second colour is available" (1.3, p.7). Fix the gray paint cite from "(7.3, 7.6)" to "(7.6, p.52)".
6. `imagery` gap and points: remove "no cropping/treatment rules" as stated. Add the placement and caption rules from 5.17 to 5.19 and the 5.10 quality line, and keep the missing-library statement.
7. Podcast typing: make the NASA podcast one type in `story` and `strengths` (`agency`).
8. Retype or drop the standardsmanual.com `press` entries: remove "The Worm" from `applications`; retype the other two as `reference`, or keep `press` only if the owner wants the reissuer counted. Drop the unused Creative Review source from `omissions`, or cite what it says.
9. `format` summary: cite where "designed by Danne & Blackburn in 1975" comes from (podcast / Wikipedia Richard Danne). The PDF names neither. Add that source to `format` `sources`, or soften with "per Danne".
10. `logo` sources: drop the Brand Center "current Logotype rules" entry (unused). In `governance`, keep at most one Brand Center example and mark it as post-1992 history.
11. designboom quote in `strengths`: add `[sic]` or quote the original "extradorinaiy" as printed, or paraphrase, rather than silently correcting it.
12. Chapter 6 `size`/`note`: record the interior section 6.2 that the p.45 text mentions (index lists only 6.1), and Ch.7 `size` should say "8 pages (7.1-7.8); 11 in the NASA scan".
