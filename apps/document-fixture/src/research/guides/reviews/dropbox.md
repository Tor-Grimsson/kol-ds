# Review: dropbox guide dossier

Reviewed 2026-10-22 against brand.dropbox.com (all 8 chapter pages and dropbox.com/branding fetched live), the Brand Partner Toolkit zip (downloaded; Partner Guidelines PDF read page by page, all 34 pages) and 14 cited press and agency pages.

## Verdict

**Needs fixes.** The guide facts (colour values, PDF rules, site quotes, chapter list) are accurate. The problems sit in the press and agency layer: two press claims in `omissions` are false, one quote is wrongly attributed, two quotes are altered, one gap says a page could not be fetched when it can, and the motion gap contradicts the guide's own Motion page.

## Chapters check

- Chapters 1-8 match the site nav exactly, in order and verbatim: Framework, Voice & Tone, Logo, Typography, Iconography, Color, Imagery, Motion. Types are right (Framework = strategy, Imagery = imagery, Iconography = icons).
- Sizes are right: Logo page has a 6-step sequence, Color has 3 categories, Imagery 4 types, Motion 4 principles.
- Ch 9 title is not verbatim. The header link reads "Brand Partner Toolkit"; "Partner Info" is the nav label. Ch 10 "Legal Branding Resources" is verbatim (header link, external page).
- Nothing invented. Missing and optional: the site home carries a stated-purpose intro ("These guidelines are meant to help you create things that live in the Dropbox ecosystem...") that repeats on every page; it is quoted in `format` but not indexed as a chapter. The PDF's own four chapters (Logo pp. 3-14, Color 15-23, Typography 24-28, Iconography 29-34) are only in `structure` points; acceptable.
- The header also links Dropbox Design and Dropbox Brand Studio; not chapters, fine.

## Wrong facts

1. `omissions` summary and last point: "Design Compass found no detail on accessibility or navigation" and "Web Designer Depot found no specifics on colour, type or voice content". Neither is in the articles; WDD says the reverse. Remove both and the `Design Compass notes 'No details on accessibility, navigation'` point.
2. `governance` point 5 and `structure` point 2 carry altered quotes (rows 26, 27).
3. `story` summary: "each with two or three sentences". The pillar texts are one or two sentences each (Clarity is one).
4. `logo` takeaway and gap say "no clear-space unit". The dossier's own point (PDF p. 12) records lockup spacing marked 1x and 1/2x of a glyph diamond. Say instead: no clear space around the logo is stated; the partner-lockup gap is shown only as unlabelled 1x / 1/2x diagram units with no text rule.
5. `voice` examples: says "four sample headlines" but lists three; the page shows five lines (adds "Centralize your content. Grow your business." and "These aren't just your files. They are pieces of your life.").
6. `type` gap: "weights by role" absent is right, but PDF p. 30 shows "Sharp Grotesk 20 Book", a named weight. Mention it.
7. `governance` gap: "No named owner". The site header links Dropbox Brand Studio; Design Compass names brand creative director Otis Rubbotham; Rive and Webflow name Theresa Ma, Associate Creative Director, Brand. There is no owner named in the guide itself; say so precisely.

## Status problems

- `colour` is `found` but its own gap lists two things the `ask` requires: proportions/ratios and contrast rules (only one text-on-colour rule exists). By the standard used for `logo` (partial for no clear space), `colour` should be `partial`. Judgement call; make the call explicit.
- `omissions` is `found` with two false press claims propping it up. After removing them it still stands on the guide checks (all absences confirmed in the PDF and site text), but add real press (Dezeen "clashing colours", Brand New title) as the outside voice.
- All other statuses are earned. `partial` on story, voice, type, imagery, elements, motion, applications, governance is right.

## Website-not-guide problems

- None lean on marketing site or CSS. The motion gap rightly avoids CSS easings, but see Closable gaps: the Motion page itself prints an easing value.
- `rule-style` and `story` use the legal page, which is the brand's own and linked from the guide header; acceptable.
- `voice` and `governance` lean on the developer branding guide (docs.dropboxapi.com), a different, integration-only guide; label it as such wherever it is used.
- `applications` source "Dropbox unveils colorful new look..." (2017 blog) supports no statement in that answer; it is the 2017 campaign, not the guide. Drop it.

## Closable gaps

1. **Motion**: the guide's /motion page displays `cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.45, 1)` and "Frame render time 16.2 ms" as labels in its demo widget. The gap says "No ... easing curves". Record this as "an easing value appears only as demo-widget text, not stated as a rule", and drop the "CSS easings are the website's" line.
2. **Governance**: the Webflow quote is fetchable (see row 31). Replace the paraphrase with the verbatim quote, delete "could not be fetched" and "paraphrase... seen only in search results".
3. **Format point 4**: credits Bijan Rafie-Tari, Theresa Ma, Michael Chiu to "the 2025 dropbox.design launch article", but that URL is not in any `sources`. dropbox.design is live and spells "Bijan Rafie-Tari" (Design Compass has "Rafi-Tari / Teresa"). Cite https://dropbox.design or reword to Design Compass's "led the project".
4. **Story**: still genuinely partial; the guide has no mission or values. Kepler lists a mission and values but for the brand, not the guide, so it does not close the gap.
5. Logo, type, colour, imagery, applications gaps: re-checked against all 34 PDF pages and the zip, absences confirmed (no clear space, minimum size, type scale, fonts, templates, imagery chapter).
