# Brief
%% thumb: link-atlascloud-is-en.png

Atlas Cloud brand brief, updated version, 13 Oct 2026. Condensed.

## Company

What Atlas Cloud sells today, where it wants to grow, and the claims and founding story the brief gives the agency to work from.

| | |
|---|---|
| What | Icelandic hosting, backup and disaster recovery. Data stays in Iceland under Icelandic law |
| Entry product | Readable backups of Jira and Confluence. First two weeks free, Atlas sets it up |
| Upsell path | Disaster recovery, then hosting, then whole environments on Atlas hardware in Iceland |
| Long term | Icelandic AI hosting and platform; foreign markets on a green-energy angle |
| Claims | ISO 27001 certified, "most secure data center in Iceland", cheaper than Icelandic providers, competitive with large foreign hosts, but "not the cheapest" |
| Story | Founded after the 2025 "Iceland Disconnected" exercise: about 80% of critical systems unreachable if the submarine cables were cut |

## Goals

Revenue and customer targets for the first and second year: the numbers the brand has to help reach by making Atlas known to Icelandic decision-makers.

| Horizon | Target |
|---|---|
| 12 months | All Icelandic decision-makers know Atlas; 15-20 new backup customers and 15-20 hosting customers; 7.5M ISK/month |
| 24 months | 15-20% of them add disaster recovery or hosting; 15M ISK/month |

## Market

The sectors Atlas targets under DORA and NIS2, the buyer who champions it internally, who it is not for, and how sales reach them.

| | |
|---|---|
| Sectors | Finance (insurers, pension funds, payments) under DORA; critical infrastructure (energy, transport, telecom, health) under NIS / NIS2; larger Jira and Confluence users |
| Best customer | Tech-literate IT or security lead who champions Atlas internally |
| Not for | Global enterprises, price hunters, teams happy on AWS |
| Sales | Direct outreach to win first meetings, then upsell |

## Agency scope

What the brief asks the agency to design from scratch, what to critique and sharpen, what to evaluate, and what stays out of scope.

| Type | Items |
|---|---|
| Design from scratch | Logo, colors, type, a "fifth element", templates (sales, presentations, roll-up, brochures, business cards, LinkedIn/Meta/Google ads), website look and UI components, brand guide |
| Critique and sharpen | Key messages, voice, values, personality |
| Evaluate | Keep or replace the name Atlas; how to talk about savings without sounding cheap |
| Out of scope | Website coding |

## Logistics

Budget, start date, the two milestone deadlines with UTmessan fixed, later work, delivery formats, and the contacts who handle and decide on the work.

| | |
|---|---|
| Budget | 2,000,000 ISK, scalable by milestone |
| Start | No later than 26 Oct 2026 |
| Milestone 1 | Website assets, logo, colors, type, key messages, first brand guide. Mid-December 2026 |
| Milestone 2 | Presentations, business cards, booth, ad templates. Before UTmessan, February 2027 (fixed) |
| Later | Brochures, motion, foreign-market adaptation |
| Formats | Figma source files, editable templates, brand guide |
| Contacts | Katla Ásgeirsdóttir (agency contact), Þórður Atlason (final decisions) |

## Changes

What the updated brief of 13 Oct 2026 changed from the first version: higher targets, a broader brand job, direct sales and a shorter horizon.

| Area | First version | Updated |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 2.5M / 6M ISK per month | 7.5M / 15M ISK per month |
| Customers at 12 months | 15-20 backup | 15-20 backup and 15-20 hosting |
| Brand job | Open the door with backups | Show the full domestic infrastructure first |
| Channels | Atlassian partners and community | Direct outreach |
| Finance sector | Banks included | Banks not listed |
| Action horizon | 6-12 months | 3-6 months |
| New | | Two weeks free, ISO 27001, price positioning, social content |
| Formats | Canva, Google Slides, PDF named | Tools no longer named |
