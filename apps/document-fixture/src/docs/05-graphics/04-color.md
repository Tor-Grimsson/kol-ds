# Color
%% thumb: ref-color-kolkrabbi-5.png

Set a base color and a harmony; the palette, brand picks and five-step ramps follow, as reference for the Atlas color system in 1.3.

## Harmony
%% thumb: ref-color-kolkrabbi-1.jpg

Set a base color and a harmony rule on KOL's color wheel. Every other section follows from these two choices, so a change here updates the whole page at once.

::embed color-harmony

## Roles
%% thumb: ref-color-aftra.png

The harmony's five roles: primary, secondary, light, dark and accent. Click a role to fill whichever brand slot or composition share is selected further down the page.

::embed color-roles

## Brand slots
%% thumb: ref-color-exmon.jpg

Four brand slots, two dark and two light. Select a slot, then click any swatch on the page to fill it; the picks also feed the composition below.

::embed color-brand

## Palette
%% thumb: ref-color-kolkrabbi-5.png

Ten hues 36 degrees apart at the base's saturation and lightness, plus blue-grey and grey ramps of ten steps. The blue-grey follows the base hue while linked.

::embed color-palette

## Ramps
%% thumb: ref-color-aftra-ramps.png

Five steps per hue from screen and multiply blends of each color over itself, lighter above and darker below. The grid is sized to fit on one screen.

::embed color-ramps

## Composition
%% thumb: ref-color-flik.jpg

The 60/30/10 rule by area: neutral, secondary and primary. It follows the brand light and harmony picks until a share is given a swatch of its own.

::embed color-composition
