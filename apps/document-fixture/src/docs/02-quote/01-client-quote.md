%% thumb: link-atlascloud-is-en.png

Date: October 2026, valid for 30 days
Client: Atlas Cloud ehf.
Contact: Katla Ásgeirsdóttir
Contractor: Þórður (Tor) Grímsson, [Kolkrabbi Vinnustofa](https://kolkrabbi.io)

A fixed-price proposal for a new brand identity for Atlas, from first workshop to a complete handoff. The work is in three milestones that fit the 2,000,000 ISK budget: **M1 Foundation** builds the identity, the website design and the initial brand guide; **M2 Applications** makes everything Atlas needs for the fair in February and hands over every file, so Atlas can use the brand without a designer; **M3 Automation**, included in the fixed fee, sets Atlas up to produce on-brand ads, social posts and documents from a short text brief.
