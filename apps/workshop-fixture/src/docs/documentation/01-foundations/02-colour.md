# Colour

Two inks and one accent. Opacity does the rest.

## Ink ladder

Ink weights step 8 · 16 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 80 · 100. Borders use **8**, body text **80**.

## The accent

Yellow, used once per view: the active tab, the focus ring, a selected row.
