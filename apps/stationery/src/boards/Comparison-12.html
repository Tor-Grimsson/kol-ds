Only Vercel documents a named type scale; for the other four, the scale had to be measured from CSS.

Vercel's Geist Typography page publishes classes from text-heading-72 down to text-heading-14 with size, line-height and tracking. Cloudflare's guide gives tracking and leading by role but not sizes; marketing sizes come from root tokens. The Rijksmuseum's styleguide names Heading 1-3, "Big text" and "Small text" without sizes. Sanity and W+K have none; their scales are read from utility classes and stylesheets. Body size is the quiet differentiator: W+K's base is 20px at weight 300, Cloudflare's is 16px, Kumo's UI body is 14px.

Visual language

Colour almost never touches type: emphasis comes from scale, grey value or inversion.

Vercel: the claim sits in gray-1000 and the proof in gray-900; coloured or gradient headline words are banned in design.md. Sanity: no coloured keywords or gradient text on the homepage; orange appears in type only on a pill hover. W+K: no coloured emphasis anywhere, only black and white inversion (the manifesto is white 45px Light on a #000 band). Rijksmuseum: emphasis by scale, weight and capitals. Cloudflare is the exception: cream type knocked out of an orange field, with a 1px #ff5e1f text-shadow so the type holds over the video, and a cream CTA pill with #1f1f1f text.

Imagery splits into the collection, the client's work, abstract renders and product shots; only Cloudflare writes its imagery rules down.
