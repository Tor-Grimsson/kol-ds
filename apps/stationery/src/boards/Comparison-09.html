Colour

Brand colour is a small number of values, and four of five could be described in one sentence.

Brand Core Source
Cloudflare 4 primaries: Tangerine #F6821F, Ruby #FF6633, Mango #FBAD41, White; 6 secondaries (documented) Brand colour
Rijksmuseum Slate #40474F "Wilmotteblauw", black, white, red #CC4C28 (documented UI); identity palette from 18 paintings Styleguide
Sanity Orange #FF5500 documented, #ff560a rendered; black #0D0E12 or #0b0b0b @sanity/logos colours
Vercel #000 / #fff plus 10-step gray; accent hues only as UI scales Geist Colors
W+K #000 / #fff plus Bootstrap greys; no documented palette (measured) styles.css

Only Cloudflare documents a full multi-colour palette, with CMYK and PMS values. W+K's has no documented palette at all. The Rijksmuseum has two: a UI set (eight named swatches) and a collection-derived "Rijksmuseum DNA" set of six colours from each of 18 paintings, which the answer reports has vanished from the UI (the researcher flags it as a tension).

Colour is allocated by area, and brands differ most on how much area the brand colour gets.

Estimates in the answers: the Rijksmuseum is about 70% artwork, 25% slate and black, under 2% red (an estimate from page composition). Sanity measured a full-page pixel histogram of 47.8% dark, 42.6% light, 6.4% mid-grey and 3.2% orange. W+K is about 50% client imagery, 20% black UI bands, 17% white, 10% #e9ecef (estimated by section height). Cloudflare measures 20.5% of the page as orange, and its guide says orange "should be present in every layout, whether it's just a small hit or full-bleed." Vercel has no brand hue: black and white carry the brand, and blue, red, amber and green are focus and status. Note the sourcing: the Rijksmuseum and W+K figures are estimates, Sanity's and Cloudflare's are measured.

Dark mode is a split: two defaults to system, one is dark-only, one is light-only, one is a user switch.

Vercel defaults to system (storage key still "zeit-theme", from the pre-2020 name). Cloudflare follows the OS on marketing with no toggle, and its dark surfaces are warm (#151414, text cream #f0e3de). Sanity defaults to light, has a footer switch, and hard-codes the first five homepage sections dark. The Rijksmuseum's main site is dark only (#40474f), while its Collection Online app adds a light theme (#efeae7). W+K is light only, with fixed black bands (no prefers-color-scheme queries).
