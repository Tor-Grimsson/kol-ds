What the gaps say

The most-missing answers are the ones that cost the brand effort to write down.

Counting statuses, not-found appears only on: voice-principles (Rijksmuseum, Sanity), logo-rules (Rijksmuseum, Sanity) and W+K's brand-guide. Questions with three or more partial answers: design-rationale (all five), origin (four), brief (three), perception (three). The pattern: what is published is what is measurable on the live site (layout, type, colour, nav, CTAs, homepage: all five found); what is missing is stated intent (rationale, brief, voice rules) and stewardship (logo rules, accessibility, brand guide). Agencies and institutions rarely publish the brief, and platform brands rarely publish the reason. A reader should expect to find "what" for any brand and to have to infer "why".
