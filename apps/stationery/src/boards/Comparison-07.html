The website

Four of five lead with a headline; the Rijksmuseum leads with the logo and W+K with someone else's work.

Headlines: Cloudflare "Everything we learned from powering 20% of the Internet—yours by default"; Vercel "Agentic Infrastructure" (48 to 64px, weight forced to 400, -3.84px tracking); Sanity "The Content Operations Platform" (60 to 112px). The Rijksmuseum's hero is a full-bleed photograph with the wordmark at 89vw as the headline; the real h1 is about 22px below it ("Visit the highlights. Book your ticket today."). W+K has no headline hero: a black 100vh slider of client video previews and only an office tag and campaign title.

Sources: Cloudflare: cloudflare.com · Rijksmuseum: rijksmuseum.nl/en · Sanity: sanity.io · Vercel: vercel.com · W+K: wk.com page data

Layout width is a statement: the platforms cap content at 1200-1400px, Sanity runs near edge to edge, and the Rijksmuseum has no maximum at all.

Cloudflare: 1200px sections inside a 1480px frame. Vercel: --ds-page-width 1400px with 24px margins, 12-column grid, 160px (208px from md) section gaps. Sanity: 12 columns with a 4px gutter, up to 1920px, with headlines sized by column span. The Rijksmuseum: a fluid 5.5vw gutter, 100vh image tiles, no max width. W+K: Bootstrap containers capped at 1360px but most sections on a fluid container with 75px side padding. The Rijksmuseum is also the one that defaults to dark (body #40474f, no light mode); W+K is light-only; Cloudflare, Sanity and Vercel ship both themes (see Colour).

Headers and CTAs: the platforms use a mega-menu and a pill; the museum and the agency use almost nothing.

Cloudflare, Sanity and Vercel put mega-menus in the header and a primary CTA at its right edge ("Start building", "Get started", "Sign Up"). The Rijksmuseum's header is static and transparent, ending in a red "Rijksmuseum tickets" button. W+K's holds a logo, a search icon and a hamburger, with no CTA, and hides on scroll; its menu is Work, About, Jobs, Contact.

Cloudflare's "Start building for free", Vercel's "Deploy now" and "Talk to sales", Sanity's "Start building", "Watch demo": all pills. Sanity's default label is IBM Plex Mono 13px uppercase; its hero overrides to sentence-case Waldenburg 24px. The Rijksmuseum's buttons are 50px tall with a 2px radius, the only place its red #cc4c28 appears. W+K has no buttons: "View work →" is a text link with a 1px underline.

Sources: Cloudflare: cloudflare.com · Rijksmuseum: css · Sanity: sanity.io · Vercel: vercel.com · W+K: wk.com

The core message: scale and speed for platforms, place or people for the others.

Cloudflare repeats "powering 20% of the Internet" and "335+ cities"; Vercel agents as builders and "Pay for compute, not idle time"; Sanity content as data; the Rijksmuseum "Home of The Night Watch" and "800 years of Dutch history"; W+K independence and "the best work of their lives". All five are found (Cloudflare, Rijksmuseum, Sanity, Vercel, W+K).
