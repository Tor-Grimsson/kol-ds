Voice

Voice is the least documented layer: two of five publish principles, and the two that do both write rules their own marketing breaks.

Cloudflare (found) has a Content Style Guide with three traits (for everyone, bold, helpful) and tone dials; its docs guide bans contractions and exclamation marks, yet the marketing site uses contractions ("Don't keep users waiting") and parenthetical jokes. Vercel (found) publishes copy rules in its Web Interface Guidelines: active voice, second person, "Prefer & over and", Title Case in product UI but sentence case on marketing pages; its own tensions answer notes the guidelines contradict each other and the site on case. Rijksmuseum and Sanity are not-found. W+K is partial: no writing guide, but ten "Wiedenisms" stored in its CMS and typed on every page ("Walk in stupid", "The work comes first").

Sources: Cloudflare: Content Style Guide · Vercel: Web Interface Guidelines · W+K: Contentful page data · Rijksmuseum: Press page · Sanity: llms.txt

In practice the platform voices are terse and claim-led, the museum is a host, and the agency understates.

Vercel's headlines rarely exceed eight words and proof lines follow the pattern "<Customer> <verb> <number> on Vercel." Sanity uses fragment triplets ("No migrations. No config files. No waiting.") and "X, not Y" contrasts. Cloudflare keeps jokes for developer pages ("No more capacity planning. Ever.") and jargon for enterprise (SASE, ZTNA, CASB). The Rijksmuseum writes in "a warm, plain, lightly playful host's voice", with questions and exclamation marks in editorial copy and blunt SEO phrasing in sales copy ("cheapest entry tickets"). W+K is first-person plural, conversational and self-deprecating ("We were founded as an ad agency that didn't like advertising"), with its humour at the edges (404, legal).

Recurring words: Cloudflare "build" and "by default" (and "Internet" always capitalised, a documented rule); Vercel "ship" and "deploy"; Sanity "content operations"; W+K "work" as a mass noun and "independent"; Rijksmuseum "masterpieces" and "Rembrandt, Vermeer and Van Gogh". "Agent" appears at Cloudflare, Vercel and Sanity in 2026 at once, which is where their positioning lines are moving (Cloudflare style guide, Vercel).
